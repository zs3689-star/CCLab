What we know so far about [ Chropha ]? Chropha is a diving/submerged habitat organism born at dawn with a striking bright pink appearance. At night it becomes orange and develops a yellow ring of light to light its way; mature adults are pale gray-pink during the day. Its most notable behavioral trait is rebellious reverse-following: attempts to direct it often cause it to go the opposite way. Its family and full taxonomy remain unknown.
